# Research Approaches

## Approaches Considered
1. **Canon survey** — books, novellas, wiki citations. Best for authoritative numbers; sparse.
2. **Battle case studies** — reconstruct ranges/timelines from depicted engagements. Reveals real engagement envelopes.
3. **Game sourcebooks** — Green Ronin RPG / Ships of the Expanse. Gives systematic stats; lower canon tier.
4. **First-principles physics** — derive plausible values (delta-v, railgun velocity, g-tolerance). Fills gaps.

## Selected Approach
All four, split into five parallel threads: propulsion/humans, weapons, battles, ship classes, physics cross-check.

## Rejected Approaches
- Author interviews as a standalone thread — folded into canon survey (low yield alone).
