# 02 — Weapons Systems (The Expanse)

Thread: ship and personal weapons, EW. Books are primary canon. Every number carries a tier tag:
`[BOOK]` `[NOVELLA]` `[SHOW]` `[RPG]` `[WIKI-unsourced]` `[FAN-CALC]` `[DERIVED]`.

**Headline finding:** the novels are deliberately light on hard weapon specs. Most "exact" numbers you see quoted online (40 mm PDCs, 5,000 m/s, 59 PDCs on the *Donnager*, 9,980 m/s railgun) come from the TV show's on-screen graphics or from the Green Ronin RPG's *Ships of the Expanse*, then get copied into the fandom wiki without tags. The book canon gives mostly ratios, counts, and qualitative behaviour. Book-derived numbers below were checked against wiki chapter citations where they exist; where I could only find a secondhand paraphrase of a book passage, the Notes column says so.

**PDC tactics in canon:**
- **Screen and overwhelm.** A good grid kills most of a salvo, and salvo size plus advanced guidance gets a few through. TV *Donnager*: 24 incoming, at least 2 hit [SHOW]. Book LF: "Slightly fewer than three hundred warheads", timed to arrive within seconds, were *all* killed by station PDCs plus *Whirlwind* missiles because they flew ballistic [BOOK: LF].
- **Close-pass PDC duels.** Ships at high closing speed trade PDC fire, and hull shredding is common [BOOK: TW].
- **Ammo is a real constraint.** In LF the Underground ships resupply "torpedoes and water and PDC rounds" from a cargo ship mid-campaign [BOOK: LF].

**Railgun canon behaviour.** The slug is unguided, so the weapon is a close-range kill shot. Turreted guns are on capital ships only; frigates and destroyers aim with the whole hull. Recoil is significant enough to work as a thruster (CB). Through-and-through hits leave pluggable holes unless they hit something critical [WIKI].

## Recommended simulator defaults

Two presets: **Book** (default) and **Show**. Where canon is silent, values are [DERIVED] and marked tunable.

**PDC (per mount)**
- Muzzle velocity: 5,000 m/s (book "several thousand"; show 5,000).
- Round mass: 25 g (CW crate) in Book mode; Show mode uses 40 mm with ~0.5 kg tungsten-cored rounds [DERIVED].
- Rate of fire: 4,000 rpm (~67 rps) [DERIVED from "thousands per minute", Phalanx-like]. Burst default 0.5 s; dispersion cone ~1 mrad [DERIVED].
- Effective intercept range: 5 km high-Pk, out to 15 km low-Pk. Anti-ship out to 20 km.
- Ammo: 20,000 rounds per mount per crate (≈5 min continuous at 4,000 rpm). Ship magazine = crates × mounts. Reload is a resupply event, not instant.
- Jam chance: 0.5% per 1,000 rounds sustained [DERIVED; show flavour].
- Mount counts: Corvette 6, *Donnager* 59, *Truman* 42, *Pulsar* 12, *Munroe* 8, *Behemoth* 170.

**Railgun**
- *Rocinante* keel gun, Book: 2 kg tungsten at 1,500 km/s (0.5% c, "measurable fraction of c") [DERIVED]. Recoil impulse 3,000 kN·s. Show: 1 kg at 9.98 km/s.
- Capital turreted guns: 5 kg at 3,000 km/s (1% c) [FAN-CALC]. KE 22.5 TJ.
- Rate of fire: keel guns 1 round / 6 s [DERIVED]; Ring Station-class 30 rpm [BOOK: BA].
- Effective vs maneuvering targets: dozens–hundreds of km (LW quote). Model as hit probability = f(target lateral accel × time-of-flight²) rather than a hard range. Unlimited range vs ballistic targets.
- Aiming: keel guns need hull alignment within ~0.1° [DERIVED]. Turn rate is RCS-limited and the flip imposes crew g-load.
- Recoil: apply Δv = m_p·v_p / m_ship to the firing ship.

**Torpedo**
- Acceleration: 150 g default, tunable 30–200 g [FAN-CALC from LW]. Must exceed ~15 g [BOOK: NG].
- Delta-v budget: 100 km/s default [DERIVED]. After burnout the torpedo goes ballistic and PDC Pk ×3 [BOOK: LF behaviour].
- Warheads:
  - Plasma: shaped thermal, armor-defeating, short standoff.
  - HE/frag: kinetic + fragment.
  - Payload bus: decoy/mech.
- Terminal guidance: evasive jink at 50 g lateral in the last 20 km [DERIVED]. Stealth-coated variants reduce PDC detection range by 50% [DERIVED].
- Tubes/magazines:
  - *Roci*: 2 tubes / 20 torps.
  - *Donnager*: 14 bays / ~90+ magazine.
  - *Pulsar*: 2×4 rails, fast reload.
  - *Raptor*: 12 bays.
  - *Xerxes*: 24 bays.
  - Tube cycle 10 s [DERIVED].

**Bombardment rock (Free Navy style)**
- Stealth-coated rocks are detectable only in the last ~1 s before impact [BOOK].

**Superweapons:** USM field projector is a scripted event, not a balanced weapon. Instant destruction of any target <~3,000,000 km [WIKI]; limited antimatter shots (3–6) [DERIVED]; firing triggers a 3-min system-wide "blackout" penalty [BOOK].

**EW**
- Jamming: blinds target sensors for up to 3 s per ambush burst [BOOK: LF].
- Stealth: only with drive off. Drive plume detection is effectively system-wide.
- Active lock: broadcasts an escalation event.

## Gaps / uncertainty

1. **No book-canon PDC caliber or rate of fire.** 40 mm and 5,000 m/s are show/wiki. The 25 g round mass (CW) conflicts with a 40 mm tungsten round. Rate of fire is only "thousands per minute" (RPG).
2. **Railgun velocity is the biggest spread in canon.** It runs from 9.98 km/s (show *Roci*) to "measurable fraction of c" (BA book) to 30–50k km/s (show RSR-G). Default 0.5–1% c is a judgment call.
3. **Torpedo acceleration and delta-v are never stated in the books.** The 150–177 g figure is a fan calc from the LW Canterbury scene; I could not verify the "200,000 km in 8 minutes" passage directly. Delta-v is fully [DERIVED].
5. **Free Navy rock mass and velocity are unstated in canon.** The default is derived from described effects.
6. **USM range (<3,000,000 km)** is an unsourced wiki infobox value.
7. **Ship weapon loadouts (bays/PDC counts)** for *Donnager*, *Truman*, *Xerxes*, *Raptor*, *Munroe*, *Andronicus*, *Asp* are almost entirely RPG (*Ships of the Expanse*), not books.
8. **Goliath ammo conflicts** (5 mm vs "2 mm at >1,000 m/s" vs TV 6.25 mm). Needs a direct CW text check.
9. **No comm-laser weapon power figure** (AG). Lasers are otherwise absent as ship weapons.
10. **Could not access Reddit or Atomic Rockets Expanse-specific pages directly** (blocked/no Expanse content). Several "book" items above are paraphrases relayed by fan pages. A pass with the actual ebooks (search "rounds", "g", "kilometers", "torpedo") would harden tiers.
