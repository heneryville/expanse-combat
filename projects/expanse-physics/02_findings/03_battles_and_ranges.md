## 1. Leviathan Wakes

### 1.1 Destruction of the *Canterbury*
The *Knight* went to investigate the *Scopuli* distress beacon. A stealth ship near the contact point fired torpedoes at the ice hauler *Canterbury* (about 750 m long, crew of about 50) and destroyed it. The *Knight* was damaged by the debris.

*Note:* "over 40 km/s and accelerating" fits a check early in a ~177 g burn, about 23 s in. Torpedoes here are drones pulling roughly 100–200 g. That is two orders of magnitude above what crewed ships sustain.

The *Donnager* is the MCRN Jupiter-fleet flagship. It recovered the *Knight*, then six Protogen stealth frigates (*Anubis*/*Amun-Ra* type) attacked it. The ships closed, jammed, and fired. Two torpedoes got through. Boarders took the ship, and the crew scuttled her. The *Tachi* (later the *Rocinante*) escaped from the hangar.

## 10. Tactical doctrine observed

1. **Torpedoes dominate long range** because they are uncrewed (an implied ~100–200 g in LW), guided, and can be massed. Defense is a matter of PDC saturation. Attackers stagger launches so everything arrives at once (Ring Station, LF) and time salvos to arrive within seconds (Laconia, LF). [WIKI citing LF]
2. **Ballistic torpedoes are easy to kill.** The roughly 300 timed but ballistic torpedoes at Laconia were all shot down. Terminal maneuvering is what lets torpedoes through PDC nets (Donnager). [WIKI]
3. **Railguns need knife range and prediction.** The *Roci* has to flip at high g to aim its keel. Bobbie fired PDCs into the *Pella*'s predicted dodge lane. Long-range railgun fire only works on static or sluggish targets such as stations, rocks, and slow-zone traffic. [WIKI citing BA, LF]
4. **PDCs do double duty.** They are missile defense first and knife-fight guns second. A 5 km/s muzzle velocity makes them useless against ships beyond tens of km. [BOOK: CW]
5. **High-g maneuvering costs people.** At around 10 g crew black out or are put out of action. Ships that pass at 8 g get only "seconds" of weapons window and need hours to come back. [WIKI citing BA]
6. **Stealth means cold.** Drive plumes are visible across the system, so stealth comes from coasting:
   - the *Roci* approaching Thoth ballistically, disguised as cargo;
   - the *Roci* approaching the *Azure Dragon* on RCS only;
   - the *Storm* leaving a cargo bay with its reactor off;
   - stealth coatings on ships and rocks;
   - liquid-hydrogen hull cooling and a "dirty" drive tune used to spoof signatures (LF).
7. **Use bodies for cover and concealment.** Jupiter blocked line of sight from Earth during the *Storm* raid. The *Roci* hid in Laconia's corona. The *Storm* hid by a moon at Freehold. The *Roci* ducked behind Thoth's spin habitat. [WIKI]
8. **Decoys and deception.**
   - fake drive signatures (the *Pella* decoy);
   - shell disguises (the *Pella* dressed as a transport);
   - feigned engine failure (*Bellerophon*);
   - drone saturation (the *Giambattista*'s ~4,000 drones);
   - jamming that forces tightbeam;
   - pre-launched loitering torpedoes waiting at a gate (*Mammatus*).
9. **Light lag shapes command.** Reports arrive hours late (Luna watching the Sol gate fight), so local commanders act on their own.
10. **Boarding is a real objective.** It happens at the *Donnager*, Thoth, the *Agatha King*, the *Azure Dragon*, the Laconian freighter, and the *Storm*, because ships, cores, and people are prizes.

## 12. Gaps and uncertainty

- **No first-hand full-text verification.** Pirated copies were excluded. The book numbers come from forum citations (page numbers vary by edition), wiki citations, or memory. Highest-value fix: someone with the ebooks searches LW ch.3/11/15, BA around p.65/190/228, and AG's slow-zone chapters for exact quotes.
- **Torpedo acceleration is never stated outright.** 177 g comes only from "200,000 km in 8 min". If the torpedoes started with closing velocity or were boosted, the figure drops.
- **Railgun velocity conflicts.** The books give "measurable fraction of c" (BA), while fans and TV give 5–10 km/s. The CB "5,000 m/s railgun" citation may be a PDC misattribution.
- **Donnager numbers are contaminated.** The 24 enemy torpedoes, 18–20 return shots, and kill counts on the wiki mix in TV details. The book says 6 attackers and a boarding, and its exact losses are unclear.
- **No book range is given for the Medina or Ring Station railguns.** Only the geometry of a 500,000 km radius is solid.
- **The USM "<3,000,000 km" figure is a wiki infobox value.** Its PR chapter citation is unchecked.
- **Thoth detection and launch ranges, Eros chase g-forces, and Io battle ranges** were not recovered.
- **Battle for the Sol gate details** (17 vs. 3 ships, 3 h lag, micrometeor cloud) come mainly from the TV-heavy wiki page. The book version is less specific.
- **Cibola Burn orbital fight specifics** (*Barbapiccola* vs. *Israel*) were not recovered beyond the railgun-as-thruster event.
- **RPG range bands** are from a fan summary and haven't been checked against the Green Ronin core rulebook's ship-combat chapter.
